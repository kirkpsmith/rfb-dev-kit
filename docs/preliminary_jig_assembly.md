# Adding pumps, controller, reservoir and tubing to jig

Gather the following components:
{{BOM}}


## Add tubing and connectors to top pump connections {pagestep}

Add two pieces of [4 cm cut tubing](fromstep){qty: 2} to the upper barbed fittings of each pump. Add a [F-31 Luer Lock Adapter][F-31 Luer Lock Adapters]{cat: part, qty: 2} to the other end of each piece of tubing.


## Add reservoirs to jig {pagestep}

Insert the [combined reservoir with tubing](fromstep){qty: 1} into the [jig with pumps and power electronics](fromstep){qty: 1} as shown, connecting the shorter tubing on the inner barbs of the reservoirs to the bottom connection of the pump. The reservoirs should "click" into place into the holding mechanism on the jig.
The flexible tubing will eventually take on a shape similar to what is shown when the cell is installed.
