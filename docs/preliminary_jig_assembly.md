## Add reservoirs to jig {pagestep}

Insert the [combined reservoir with tubing](fromstep){qty: 1} into the [jig with pumps and power electronics](fromstep){qty: 1} as shown, connecting the shorter tubing on the inner barbs of the reservoirs to the bottom connection of the pump.
