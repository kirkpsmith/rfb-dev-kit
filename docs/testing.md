# Operating and testing the cell

{{BOM}}

## Weigh and add electrolyte to the cell{pagestep}

Using a [5 mL syringe]{qty: 1,cat: tool, Note: "or larger"} and the [scale]{qty: 1,cat: tool}, add 5 mL of [prepared electrolyte](fromstep) to each reservoir, weighing the syringe before and after injection to record the actual mass added to each reservoir. 

## Close the reservoirs{pagestep}

Carefully insert 19/22 silicone [septa][19/22 silicone septa](septa.md){qty: 2,cat: part} into the reservoirs and fold them over to seal them.

![](images/Screenshot_20260731_175311.png)



## Pre-circulate the electrolyte{pagestep}

Turn the pumps on and circulate the electrolytes for 15 min before applying any current. 


>i **Note** 
>i
>i This should allow enough time for the separator to become fully wetted with electrolyte and clear out any bubbles trapped in the cell/felts.

## Connect to the potentiostat and define cycling conditions{pagestep}


>!! **Warning** 
>!!
>!! Enclose the battery when cycling - you can put it inside a plastic tub - because leaks due to any problem will spray charged electrolyte, which, even if the volume is low, can be dangerous.
