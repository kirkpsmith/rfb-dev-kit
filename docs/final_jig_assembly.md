# Attaching the assembled cell to the jig
Gather the following components:
{{BOM}}

## Rotate the assembled cell away from the jig and connect lower tubing connections {pagestep}

Take the [assembled flow cell](fromstep){qty:1} and place it into the [assembled jig](fromstep){qty: 1}. Rotate the cell so the clamp is facing up; this makes it easier to connect the lower tubing connections first. Connect the lower tubing of the cell to the 9 cm tubing length pieces coming from the reservoir outlets as shown, twisting together the Luer Lock fittings tightly to seal.

![](images/Screenshot_20260731_171517.png)
![](images/Screenshot_20260731_171601.png)
![](images/Screenshot_20260731_172039.png)




## Rotate the cell towards the jig and connect upper tubing connections {pagestep}

Now that the lower tubing Luer fittings are connected, rotate the cell upwards and connect the remaining fittings as shown.

![](images/Screenshot_20260731_172240.png)
![](images/Screenshot_20260731_172357.png)
![](images/Screenshot_20260731_172332.png)





>!! **Warning** 
>!!
>!! Avoid creating kinks and sharp bends in the tubing when assembling the cell. This can create issues with electrolyte flow.



>!! **Warning** 
>!!
>!! Electrolyte flow must flow from bottom of the cell upwards to top, in order to clear out gas bubbles from the cell and ensure good liquid electrolyte flow



## Place the jig into secondary containment {pagestep}

Place the entire assembled jig into a [secondary container]{cat: part, qty: 1, Note: "Gastronorm 1:3 150mm deep polypropylene works well"}

The kit has been designed to fit into a GN 1:3 150 mm deep plastic container.
